Easy Manage

Digital Ledger, Cashbook & Customer Khata Application

An intuitive, offline-first mobile app designed for small businesses, shopkeepers, and individuals to manage customer balances, daily income/expenses, and secure Google Drive backups.

About Easy Manage & Application Purpose

Easy Manage is an Android mobile bookkeeping application created by developer Usama Ali. The purpose of the application is to replace traditional paper-based Khata notebooks with a fast, private, and 100% digital solution.

All transaction calculations and record management run locally on the user's device without requiring a server connection. Users have full ownership of their data and can optionally connect their Google Account to create private, encrypted backups to their personal Google Drive storage.

📒

Customer & Supplier Ledger

Track credits (Udhaar) and debits (Wasool) with instant balance calculation and PDF invoice generation.

💵

Daily Cashbook Management

Monitor daily cash in and cash out with automatic daily balances and expense categorisation.

☁️

Google Drive Cloud Backup

Safeguard ledger records with 1-tap backup and restore directly connected to the user's Google Drive.

Google APIs Terms of Service & Policy Compliance

Strict Adherence to Google API Policies: Easy Manage complies fully with the Google APIs Terms of Service and Google API Services User Data Policy.

The application is solely a financial recordkeeping and ledger tool. It uses Google Drive APIs (drive.appdata and drive.file) exclusively for backing up user-generated financial spreadsheets and ledger databases. It does not use Google APIs for AI generation, image processing, or any prohibited content.

Privacy, Security & Data Handling

Easy Manage does not collect, sell, or transmit financial records to third-party databases. When a user requests a Google Drive backup, the app connects directly to the user's private Google Drive storage space using the drive.appdata and drive.file OAuth scopes.
