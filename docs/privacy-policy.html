Privacy Policy for Easy Manage

Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective Immediately

This Privacy Policy outlines how Easy Manage ("we", "our", or "the Application"), developed by Usama Ali, collects, processes, stores, and protects user data. Easy Manage is an Android mobile bookkeeping and digital ledger application.

1. App Identity and Ownership

The Application name is strictly Easy Manage. It is designed to assist users with personal and business record keeping, customer ledger management, and cashbook tracking.

2. Types of Data Processed

Easy Manage is engineered with an offline-first architecture:

  • Ledger & Transaction Information: Customer and supplier contact names, phone numbers, transaction amounts, credit/debit balances, cashbook entries, and calculation records. All this data is stored locally on the user's mobile device using local device storage.
  • No Third-Party Analytics Tracking: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transmit your ledger records to external servers, advertisers, or third-party brokers.

3. Google Drive Integration & OAuth Scopes

Easy Manage provides an optional feature allowing users to back up and restore their data using their personal Google Account.

Disclosure of Google OAuth 2.0 Scopes:
When you enable Google Drive Backup, Easy Manage requests the following scopes:
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata (Application Data Folder): Used exclusively to save and retrieve your encrypted backup file (easymanage_ledger_backup.json) in the private, hidden app folder on your own Google Drive.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file: Used to create and manage only the backup files specifically created by Easy Manage.
What We Do NOT Access: Easy Manage cannot access, read, modify, or delete any other documents, photos, or files in your Google Drive.

4. Google API Limited Use Requirements Compliance

Easy Manage’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We do not transfer user data to third parties, except as strictly necessary to provide the backup feature requested by the user.
  • We do not use user data for serving advertisements, retargeting, or personalized marketing.
  • We do not allow humans to read user data unless required for security investigations, compliance with law, or with the user's explicit consent for troubleshooting.

5. Data Security and Encryption

All communications between Easy Manage and Google Drive APIs are protected using industry-standard SSL/TLS HTTPS encryption during transit. Backup payloads are saved directly to the user's private Google account storage.

6. Data Retention and Deletion Rights

Users maintain complete autonomy over their data:

  • Local Data Deletion: You can delete all local records at any time using the "Reset Ledger Data" option inside the app settings or by clearing app data.
  • Cloud Backup Deletion: You can delete your cloud backup directly from the Google Drive Backup screen in the app, or revoke access at any time through your Google Security Settings at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
