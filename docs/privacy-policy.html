Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective Immediately

Welcome to EasyManage ("we", "our", or "the App"). EasyManage is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your data when you use our offline bookkeeping, digital ledger, and Google Drive cloud backup services.

1. Data Collection and Local Storage

EasyManage is designed with a privacy-first, offline-first architecture. All your business entries, customer ledger records, suppliers, credit/debit transactions, cashbook entries, and calculation records are stored strictly locally on your device.

  • We do not operate central database servers that store or read your financial ledger entries.
  • We do not track, sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data to any third parties.

2. Google Drive Cloud Backup & OAuth Access

EasyManage provides an optional feature allowing users to create encrypted backups of their ledger to their own personal Google Drive account.

Google User Data Policy Compliance:
EasyManage requests access to the following Google OAuth scopes:
  • drive.appdata: Used exclusively to create, read, and update your encrypted backup file (easymanage_ledger_backup.json) in your personal Google Drive hidden Application Data folder.
  • drive.file: Used to manage only files opened or created by EasyManage.
EasyManage does not read, modify, or delete any other personal files, photos, or documents stored in your Google Drive.

3. Use of Google User Data

EasyManage adheres strictly to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We only use Google authentication tokens to authenticate your device with Google Drive APIs.
  • Google user data is never transferred to external servers, advertising networks, or data brokers.
  • Data transferred between the App and Google Drive is encrypted in transit using industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption.

4. Data Ownership & Deletion

You retain 100% full ownership of your data at all times. You can delete your local data by clearing the app cache/storage or uninstalling the app. You can delete your cloud backup directly within the app's Google Drive Settings screen or by disconnecting the app in your Google Account security dashboard at Google Permissions.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
